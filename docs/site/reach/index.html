在外面也能连Reach your desktop from anywhere

SuperLeo 装在你的电脑上，手机连接这台电脑。 SuperLeo runs on your computer. Your phone connects to that computer.

怎么连过去How the phone gets there

  • 同一个 Wi-FiSame Wi-Fi 手机直接连电脑，不经过任何别的机器。The phone connects to the computer directly. Nothing else is involved.
  • 在别的地方Anywhere else 经过 relay.superleo.app 中转。不用注册。Through relay.superleo.app. No sign-up.
  • 你自己的方式Your own way 自己跑一个中转，或者用你已有的隧道。见下文。Run a relay yourself, or use a tunnel you already have. See below.

中转看不到内容The relay cannot read anything

配对时，手机从二维码里拿到电脑证书的指纹。之后每次连接，加密都是在手机和电脑之间完成的，手机只认这张证书。中转只按地址把加密后的数据转给对应的电脑，看不到内容，也不保存。 When you pair, the phone takes the fingerprint of the computer's certificate from the QR code. Every connection after that is encrypted between the phone and the computer, and the phone accepts only that certificate. The relay forwards the encrypted bytes to the right computer by name. It cannot read them and does not store them.

每台电脑在中转上的地址是用它自己的密钥算出来的，形如 https://<id>.relay.superleo.app:43443，<id> 是 16 个字符。电脑连上中转时用同一把密钥签名，证明这个地址属于它，所以不需要账号。 Each computer's address at the relay is derived from its own key and looks like https://<id>.relay.superleo.app:43443, where <id> is 16 characters. When the computer connects to the relay it signs with that key to prove the address is its own, so no account is needed.

桌面版的「设置 › 通知中心 › 在外面也能连」里能看到这台电脑的中转地址，以及它现在有没有连上中转。 The desktop app shows this computer's relay address, and whether it is linked to the relay, under Settings › Notifications › Away from home.

换成你自己的方式Use your own provider

在同一个设置里可以选：SuperLeo 中转、自己的中转、或者仅局域网。另外可以加上你自己的地址，手机会依次尝试：先局域网，再你自己的地址，最后中转。 The same settings let you choose the SuperLeo relay, your own relay, or the local network only. You can also add addresses of your own. The phone tries them in order: the local network first, then your own addresses, then the relay.

自己跑一个中转Run the relay yourself

中转是开源的，代码在 cmd/superleo-relay。准备一个域名，加一条泛解析 *.relay.example.com 指向你的服务器，然后运行： The relay is open source, in cmd/superleo-relay. Point a wildcard DNS record, *.relay.example.com, at your server and run: 

superleo-relay -addr :43443 -domain relay.example.com

在桌面版选「自己的中转」，填 relay.example.com:43443。中转需要域名，不能只用 IP 地址，因为每台电脑都在它下面有一个自己的子域名。 In the desktop app choose My own relay and enter relay.example.com:43443. A relay needs a domain name, not just an IP address, because each computer gets a subdomain of its own under it.

用你已有的隧道Use a tunnel you already have

按 TCP 原样转发的隧道都可以，比如 frp 的 TCP 代理，或者 Tailscale Funnel 的 TCP 转发。把它指向电脑的 43561 端口，再把它给你的地址（例如 https://desk.example.com:7443）加到「自己的地址」里。手机也装了 Tailscale 的话，可以直接加电脑的 Tailscale 地址，例如 https://100.101.102.103:43561。 Any tunnel that passes TCP through unchanged works, such as an frp TCP proxy or Tailscale Funnel's TCP forwarding. Point it at port 43561 on the computer, and add the address it gives you (for example https://desk.example.com:7443) under Own addresses. If your phone is on Tailscale too, you can add the computer's Tailscale address, for example https://100.101.102.103:43561.

在中间解开 TLS 的隧道不支持，比如 Cloudflare Tunnel。它能读到经过的内容，手机核对证书指纹时也会失败。 Tunnels that terminate TLS, such as Cloudflare Tunnel, are not supported. They can read the traffic, and the phone's certificate check fails through them.

目前能用到哪里What works today

桌面版已支持自己的中转和自己的地址。iPhone 和 Android 版从下一个版本开始支持；在此之前，手机在外面使用 SuperLeo 中转。 The desktop app supports your own relay and your own addresses. The iPhone and Android apps support them from their next versions. Until then, phones use the SuperLeo relay away from home.
