在外面也能连Reach your desktop from anywhere

SuperLeo 跑在你的电脑上，手机连的是这台电脑。 SuperLeo runs on your computer. Your phone talks to that computer.

手机怎么连过去How the phone gets there

  • 同一个 Wi-FiSame Wi-Fi 直接连电脑。Straight to the computer.
  • Tailscale 手机和电脑在同一个 tailnet 里，就走 Tailscale，不用设置。If the phone and the computer are on the same tailnet, it goes over Tailscale. Nothing to set up.
  • 其他情况Anywhere else 经 gateway.superleo.cn 或 gateway.superleo.app 中转。不用注册。Through gateway.superleo.cn or gateway.superleo.app. No sign-up.

中转看不到内容The relay can't read anything

配对时手机记下电脑证书的指纹，之后只认这张证书。加密在手机和电脑之间完成，中转只负责转发，看不到内容，也不保存。电脑用自己的密钥证明身份，所以不需要账号。 When you pair, the phone keeps the fingerprint of the computer's certificate and trusts only that. Encryption runs between the phone and the computer; the relay just passes bytes along, can't read them and keeps nothing. The computer proves who it is with its own key, so there's no account.

用你自己的Use your own

在桌面版「设置 › 通知中心 › 在外面也能连」里换。 Change it in the desktop app under Settings › Notifications › Away from home.

自己的中转Your own relay

中转是开源的（cmd/superleo-relay）。把 *.relay.example.com 解析到你的服务器，运行： The relay is open source (cmd/superleo-relay). Point *.relay.example.com at your server and run: 

superleo-relay -addr :43443 -domain relay.example.com

然后在桌面版填 relay.example.com:43443。 Then enter relay.example.com:43443 in the desktop app.

自己的隧道Your own tunnel

frp 的 TCP 代理这类原样转发 TCP 的隧道都行：指向电脑的 43561 端口，把得到的地址加到「自己的地址」里。 Any tunnel that passes TCP through as is, like an frp TCP proxy, works: point it at port 43561 on the computer and add the address it gives you under Own addresses.

Cloudflare Tunnel 这类会解开 TLS 的隧道不行，手机的证书校验过不去。 Tunnels that open up TLS, like Cloudflare Tunnel, don't work: the phone's certificate check fails.
