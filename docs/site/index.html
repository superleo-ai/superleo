SuperLeo

一个装在自己电脑上的 AI 助手。 An AI assistant you install on your own computer.

我想要一个能记住我的日程、能替我跑腿的助手，但不想把这些交给别人的服务器。所以写了 SuperLeo。它装在你自己的电脑上，手机和手表连回这台电脑，对话和记录都留在你这里。代码是开源的，哪里不放心可以直接去看。 I wanted an assistant that keeps track of my schedule and runs errands for me, without handing all of that to someone else's servers. So I wrote SuperLeo. It lives on your own computer, your phone and watch connect back to it, and your conversations and records stay with you. The code is open, so you can read exactly what it does.

平时怎么用How I use it

早上它把今天的日程和待办整理好推到手机上。临时想起一件事，对着手表说一句，它会记下来，到时间提醒我。 In the morning it sends today's schedule and to-dos to my phone. When something comes to mind, I say it to my watch, and it reminds me at the right time.

要查资料、改代码、看行情这类花时间的事，我交代给它，它会分给合适的机器去做，做完了告诉我结果。需要我确认的事，它会先问我。 Slower jobs, like looking things up, changing code or checking the markets, I hand to it, and it gives them to whichever machine suits them best and reports back. Anything that needs my approval, it asks first.

背后用哪家的模型由你自己决定，兼容 OpenAI 接口的服务都可以接，也可以接你自己跑的模型。 You choose the model behind it. Any service with an OpenAI-compatible API works, including models you run yourself.
